Before you use it
  • Use only these free-licence libraries, or stock you have actually bought. Do not pull images off a search engine, a competitor's site, or a social feed — those are someone's copyright, and a takedown lands on the client.
  • Check the licence on the individual photo, not the site. A few are editorial-only and cannot be used commercially.
  • Recognisable faces and private property need a model or property release for advertising use.
  • Record the source URL, photographer and licence next to the asset. PHOTOS.md has the line to keep.
  • Never use an AI-upscaled or watermark-removed version of a paid preview.
