🔒 What the shopper never does

No account. No password. No card on our site. We lose: their contact details, abandoned-cart chasing, and any email list. We gain: no card data, no refunds, no payment licence, and no liability for a sale we were never part of.

The licence customer's journey

8 steps

From no trade licence to a store that is legally selling.

Step 1

They decide

They are selling from an Instagram account, or want to start, and cannot do it legally.

🧾 The problem

  1. L1

    No trade licence

    Trading in the UAE needs a valid licence, including selling through social media.

  2. L2

    They pick تأسيس كامل

    1,500 AED once: licence, built store, first products, training, a full year of Store.

Step 2

The call, then the papers

Nothing is charged before they know the real number.

☎️ First call — free

  1. L3

    Twenty minutes

    Emirate, licence type, activity — and the CONFIRMED government fee before they pay anything.

📄 What they send

  1. L4

    Documents and name

    Passport, photo, three proposed trade names, activity description. We reserve the name.

Step 3

Filing

This is the step you cannot do without a partner.

🏛️ With the authority

  1. L5

    We file it

    Through a LICENSED SETUP PARTNER. They pay government fees to the authority directly.

  2. L6

    Licence issued

    Usually 3–10 working days once documents are complete. The AUTHORITY issues it, not us.

Step 4

They open

Everything they were paying for, delivered at once.

🏪 We build it all

  1. L7

    Page, store, products

    First 20 products uploaded, then an hour of training on running it.

  2. L8

    A year before the next payment

    The monthly clock starts after that year, and only starts once the licence exists.

⚠️ Two promises that must never be edited out

1. We do not issue licences. The government authority does. We prepare, file, and chase — through a licensed partner. 2. No fee number before the call. Government fees differ by emirate, licence type and activity, and they change. Promising an outcome you do not control is how setup agents collect complaints.

🚧 This path is blocked today

L5 is the gate. Without a licensed setup partner you cannot legally file for anyone, so the 1,500 service cannot be sold yet — while the 55 store can be sold today.
