The merchant's journey

M1–M12

From first hearing about us to their second month.

  1. M1

    They hear about us

    An ad, another merchant's referral, a QR sticker on a shop or a delivery bag.

  2. M2

    They see a real souq

  3. M3

    They register free

  5. M5

    matjari.ae/@handle — links, socials, WhatsApp button. Ready for their bio today.

  10. M10

  11. M11

    Orders reach their phone

    Items, total, and the customer's number on WhatsApp. They deliver and are paid directly.

  12. M12

    Month two onward

    Reminder 3 days before, link on the day, warning if late. Unpaid = store off, page and products stay.

Free, and useful immediately

M1–M6 cost the merchant nothing. They walk away with a page they can use in their Instagram bio the same day. Why give it away: a free page keeps them somewhere we can reach them, and it is the cheapest proof that we do what we say.

Where the money starts

M7–M10 is the whole business: they pay, we build, we feature them. The rule that protects you: the store only opens on a cleared payment, never on a request.

What keeps them

M11–M12: orders arriving on their phone is the habit. The renewal cycle runs itself, and suspension takes the store — never the page, the links, or the products.

The shopper's journey

S1–S6

From arriving at the souq to paying a merchant.

  1. S1

    They arrive

    From search, a merchant's bio link, our social accounts, or a QR sticker in a real shop.

  2. S2

    They browse the souq

    Featured stores, categories, every store with search. Many merchants in one place is the reason to return.

  3. S3

    They open a store

    That merchant's store, inside our website, carrying our chrome and their brand.

  4. S4

    They open a product

    Photos, price, what's included, quantity.

  5. S5

    They add to cart

    Optionally leave a name and WhatsApp number so the merchant can reply.

  6. S6

    They pay the merchant

    Checkout opens WhatsApp to THE MERCHANT. Delivery and payment agreed there. No account, no card on our site.

The shopper never meets the platform

No login, no password, no saved card. They browse, they message a merchant, they pay that merchant. What this costs us: we cannot see their orders, chase abandoned carts, or email them later. What it buys us: no card data, no refunds, no payment licence, and no liability for a sale we were not part of.

The licence customer's journey

L1–L8

From no trade licence to a store that is legally selling.

  1. L1

    No trade licence

    Selling from an Instagram account, or wanting to start, and unable to do it legally.

  2. L2

    They pick تأسيس كامل

    1,500 AED once: licence, built store, first products, training, and a full year of Store.

  3. L3

    Free 20-minute call

    Emirate, licence type, activity — and the CONFIRMED government fee before they pay anything.

  4. L4

    Documents and name

    Passport, photo, three proposed trade names, activity description. We reserve the name.

  5. L5

    We file it

    Through a LICENSED SETUP PARTNER. They pay government fees to the authority directly.

  6. L6

    Licence issued

    Usually 3–10 working days once documents are complete. The AUTHORITY issues it, not us.

  7. L7

    We build everything

    Page, store, first 20 products, then an hour of training on running it.

  8. L8

    A year before the next payment

    The monthly clock starts after that year — and only starts once the licence exists.

Two promises that must never be edited out

1. We do not issue licences. The government authority does. We prepare, file, and chase — through a licensed partner. 2. No fee number before the call. Government fees differ by emirate, licence type and activity, and they change. The confirmed figure comes on the first call, before any payment. Promising an outcome you do not control is how setup agents collect complaints.

This path is blocked until you have a partner

L5 is the gate. Without a licensed setup partner you cannot legally file for anyone, so the 1,500 service cannot be sold — while the 55 store can be sold today.

Your week

O1–O7

What lands on you, what runs without you, and where it stops scaling.

  1. O1

    Signups land on your phone

    You build each free page from the sheet row. Minutes each.

  2. O2

    Store requests handle themselves

    The payment link goes out automatically. You do nothing until money lands.

  3. O3

    You build the paid store

    THE REAL WORK. Hours per merchant, and the reason they pay you instead of doing it themselves.

  4. O4

    Setup cases

    The call, the documents, the filing, and four stage updates that send themselves once you post each stage.

  5. O5

    Marketing

    Rotate who is featured, post each new store, run the seasonal campaigns.

  6. O6

    Support

    Order questions, product help, pricing advice — in working hours, which is what we promise.

  7. O7

    Renewals run without you

    Reminders, warnings and suspensions are automatic. You step in only for the ones that go quiet.

The one that does not scale

O3 is the bottleneck. Every paid store costs you hours of hand-building. Ten merchants is a good week; fifty is not a person's job. That single node is the argument for building the real backend — not features, not design. When O3 stops fitting in your week, self-serve editing pays for itself.

What the automations already carry

O2 and O7 are done by workflows 02 and 03. You are not chasing payments or watching dates — you are building stores and answering merchants.
