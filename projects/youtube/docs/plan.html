Grow the channel into an income source

Organize what's shot, calendar it, confirm every item before it goes out, then let it publish on schedule. Growth first — the monetization switches are the last step, not the first.

⚠️ This is the plan, not a running automation. It shows the shape of the pipeline — no video, schedule, or post exists yet. Answer the open questions at the bottom, then it's ready to build for real (e.g. as an n8n workflow or a shared calendar + upload tool).
Step 1

Set the foundation

Everything downstream — the calendar, the edits, the growth push — assumes these are already decided.

🎯 Define the channel

  • Confirm the niche

    The topic and the audience the channel is for. Every pillar in Step 3 should trace back to this.

  • Baseline the numbers

    Current subscribers, average views, watch hours — the line everything else is measured against.

🎨 Brand it

  • Channel art & bio

    Banner, profile image, description, links — the shop window.

  • Pick 3–5 content pillars

    Recurring themes so the calendar in Step 3 has categories to fill, not a blank page.

Step 2

Collect and log the raw material

The bottleneck for most channels: footage exists but is scattered. One library, one naming rule, logged automatically.

📥 Bring it in

  • Shoot / gather footage

    Video and photo, tagged at the point of capture with the pillar it belongs to.

  • Drop into the library

    One shared folder, named by date + pillar, so nothing is "somewhere on someone's phone".

✂️ Log it

  • Sort by format

    Long-form vs. Shorts vs. photo/community post — guessed from the filename, correctable in the calendar.

  • Row added, status "Needs edit"

    Picked up automatically by the auto-edit step next — no manual cutting required.

Step 2b

Auto-edit, enhance, add a soundtrack

Not an n8n workflow — this step runs when you ping Claude in chat. Claude generates a soundtrack and assembles the cut with vidIQ's tools, then reads/writes the row directly through a Google Drive + Sheets connector.

🎬 Assemble automatically

  • Generate a soundtrack

    A short generated track matched to the video's pillar/mood (vidiq_generate_music), made before assembly so it can be baked into the compose step.

  • Compose the cut

    The raw clip, the generated soundtrack, and a title overlay are composited by vidIQ's compose tool into a rendered MP4 — mechanical assembly, not creative editing.

🎯 Sharpen the metadata

  • Generate title & thumbnail options

    Scored candidates (vidiq_generate_titles/_thumbnail) ready for the human pick in the next step — not auto-applied.

  • File as Ready to schedule

    Dropped into the Edited folder and logged back to the calendar by Claude — nothing left but the final title choice and the publish date.

Step 3

Build the posting calendar

The one thing still done by hand: picking a title and a publish date for each auto-edited item.

🗓️ Fill the queue

  • Slot into the calendar

    Mix the pillars across the week; place Shorts on high-frequency days, long-form on fewer, bigger days.

  • Write title, tags, description

    The SEO pass — done once per item, in the calendar tool, not improvised at publish time.

Step 4

Confirm before anything goes out

The one mandatory human checkpoint. Nothing in Step 5 fires until this gate is passed.

✅ Review gate

  • Preview each item

    Watch it back, check the thumbnail and title against the channel's actual voice.

  • Approve or send back

    Approved = queued for its scheduled time. Sent back = returns to Step 2 for another edit pass.

Step 5

Publish on schedule

Once approved, this runs without anyone watching the clock.

🚀 Auto-publish

  • Goes live at its scheduled time

    YouTube upload set to the calendar slot from Step 3 — no manual click needed.

  • Cross-post the cut-downs

    Shorts / clips mirrored to other platforms the same day, pointing back to the channel.

📊 Feed the numbers back

  • Watch retention & subscriber delta

    Per-video performance flows back into which pillars get more slots next week — Step 1's baseline, updated.

Step 6

Grow it, then earn from it

Growth first — income follows the audience, not the other way round.

🤝 Compound the audience

  • Reply & community posts

    Comments and community tab in the hours after publish — this is what the algorithm rewards early.

  • Collabs & cross-promo

    Borrow another channel's audience in exchange for the same back.

💰 Turn on income

  • Hit Partner Program eligibility

    1,000 subscribers + 4,000 public watch hours in 12 months, OR 10M Shorts views in 90 days — YouTube's current thresholds.

  • Turn on ads

    The first, lowest-effort income switch once eligible.

🧪 Other income, once the audience exists

  • Sponsorships outreach optional

    Brands pay for a mention once view counts make it worth their time.

  • Affiliate links optional

    Description links to products already used on camera.

📌 Numbers this plan relies on

YouTube Partner Program (2026 thresholds): 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 public watch hours in the trailing 12 months, or 10M Shorts views in the trailing 90 days.

❓ Open questions — answer before Step 1 is real

  • What is the channel's niche/topic?
  • Current subscribers, views, watch hours today?
  • Who shoots vs. edits vs. approves — one person or several?
  • How many uploads a week is sustainable?
