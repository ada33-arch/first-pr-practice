L · The licence customer's path merchant

L1

Licence, store built for them, first products uploaded, training, and a full year of Store.

L3

A free 20-minute call first

Emirate, licence type, activity — and the confirmed government fee before they pay anything. We never quote one from memory.

L4

Documents and trade name

Passport, photo, three proposed names, activity description. We reserve the name.

L5

They pay the government fees to the authority directly. We do not issue licences — the authority does.

L6

Usually 3–10 working days once documents are complete. Any delay, they hear it from us first.

L7

We build everything

Page, store, first 20 products, then an hour of training on running it.

L8

The monthly clock starts after that year — and only starts at all once the licence is issued.

A · What I decided for you

These are the things you never actually said. I chose them to keep building — every one is a real decision, and any of them could be wrong. This is the section to argue with.

A1

The front door belongs to shoppers

The homepage is the marketplace; merchants have their own page behind a افتح متجرك button. Reversible: the homepage could sell to merchants instead, with the souq one click in.

A2

The name is رنت ستور / rentstore.ae

Yours — the domain is owned. The Arabic spelling رنت ستور is mine to change on one line, and the trade name still has to be cleared when the licence is filed.

A3

"Personal page" means a link page

Links, socials, a WhatsApp button — not a profile with posts, followers, or a feed.

A4

Merchants live inside our domain

rentstore.ae/@handle, not their own domain. Their address advertises us.

A5

No commission, ever

We charge the subscription and the setup fee and nothing else, however much they sell.

A6

Money never passes through us

The customer pays the merchant directly — cash on delivery, transfer, or the merchant's own link. We hold no funds and process no cards.

A7

We build every store by hand

Merchants cannot edit their own products yet. That is a service now and a feature later.

A8

Delivery and returns are the merchant's

We ship nothing, store nothing, and refund nothing. Every order is the merchant's sale.

A9

You approve each store before it is listed

Nobody appears in the marketplace automatically, so a bad store cannot damage the others.

A10

Shoppers need no account

No login, no password, no saved cards — browse and message the merchant.

A11

UAE only, for now

Prices in AED, licences under UAE authorities, delivery inside the country.

A12

Arabic first, English second

Arabic is the default and English is a toggle — not the other way round.

A13

The perfume shop is only a demo

I used your real name and number on it. Is it actually your business — are you merchant number one? That changes how we show it.

A14

A free page stays free forever

Someone who never opens a store keeps their page, and stays somewhere we can reach them.

A15

Suspension hides the store only

Page, links, and products survive; one payment brings the store back exactly as it was. Nothing is ever deleted.

A16

"Security" means hosting and hygiene

HTTPS, maintenance, backups, verified badges, filtering fake orders. Not certifications, not insurance, not a guarantee.

A17

"Marketing" means our audience, not ad budget

Featured slots, our social accounts, campaigns, SEO. Paid ads are the merchant's own money.

A18

The setup fee includes a full year

1,500 covers licence help, the built store, and twelve months of Store — the clock starting when the licence is issued.

A19

Support is working hours

One person answering. We promise what one person can keep.

A20

We are never the seller

Not a shop, not a courier, not a payment processor. On every order the merchant is the seller of record, and the page says so.

The two things I could not decide for you

The setup partner

  • You cannot legally file a licence for someone without a licensed setup partner
  • Until one exists, the 1,500 service cannot be sold — the 55 store can
  • It also decides which emirates and licence types you can offer

Real photographs

  • A marketplace is judged on its pictures before its words
  • Drawn artwork holds the space, but six stores with no photography reads as empty
  • One shot per product per merchant is the minimum to look real
