What happens after every button

1 · Someone signs up, free

01-signup-free.json
Trigger The signup form POSTs to /webhook/rentstore-signup · nobody pays anything in this flow
  1. 1

    They fill in four fields

    Signup submitted

    Name, handle, WhatsApp number, and what they want to sell. The plan radio decides whether this ends here or continues into flow 2.

    Payload
    { "name": "أحمد العامري", "handle": "ahmed", "phone": "0508400886", "sells": "عطور", "plan": "free" }
  2. 2

    The data gets cleaned before anything trusts it

    Validate and normalise

    Ahmed.Alameri!! becomes ahmed.alameri. 0508400886 becomes 971508400886, so WhatsApp can actually reach it. A name under two characters or a broken number stops here.

  3. ✕

    Bad input turns back

    Reject: missing fields

    The site gets 400 with the list of what was wrong, and shows it. Nothing is written.

  4. 3

    Is the handle already someone's?

    Look up the handle

    A lookup against the sellers sheet. Taken means 409 and the site asks them to pick another — this is the one check that has to happen before the row exists, because two people on one handle is one broken page.

  5. 4

    The account exists

    Create the seller row

    One row: plan=free, status=active, store_status=off, and their page URL. That row is the account — every later flow reads and updates it.

  6. 5

    Both of you hear about it, at once

    WhatsApp ×2
    Seller receives
    أهلاً أحمد العامري 👋 تم تسجيلك في رنت ستور. صفحتك: rentstore.ae/@ahmed الخطوة الجاية: أرسل لنا صورتك، نبذة قصيرة، وروابط حساباتك — ونجهّز صفحتك خلال ٢٤ ساعة. وإذا حبيت تضيف متجر ومنتجات، رد بكلمة (متجر) ونرسل لك التفاصيل.
    You receive
    🆕 تسجيل جديد الاسم: أحمد العامري الرابط: rentstore.ae/@ahmed واتساب: 971508400886 يبيع: عطور الباقة المطلوبة: مجاني
  7. 6

    And the page confirms

    Reply: account created

    The site gets { ok: true, page_url } back. Your only manual job starts here: build their page from the row.

The seller ends with

  • A confirmed handle nobody else can take
  • Their page URL
  • A clear next step, and the word that starts flow 2

You end with

  • A row in the sheet
  • Their number, in an open 24-hour window
  • One page to build by hand

2 · They want a store

02-store-activation.json
Two triggers /webhook/rentstore-store-request then, later, /webhook/rentstore-stripe
  1. 1

    The request arrives

    Store requested

    Either they picked the store plan at signup, or they replied متجر later and you fire the webhook. Either way it needs only their handle.

  2. 2

    The seller has to exist first

    Find the seller

    No row, no store — 404. Flow 1 is a hard prerequisite, which is why a store can never exist without an account behind it.

  3. 3

    A real payment link, made on the spot

    Create the payment link

    Stripe creates a link for the recurring monthly price, tagged with their handle so the callback knows whose store to switch on. The row goes to awaiting_payment — requested, not active.

  4. 4

    The link goes out

    WhatsApp: send the payment link
    Seller receives
    تفعيل المتجر 🛍️ المتجر: 55 د.إ شهرياً، تقدر توقفه في أي وقت. اللي بيوصلك: • منتجات بلا حد مع صور وأسعار • صفحة لكل منتج وتصنيفات وبحث • سلة وطلبات توصلك واتساب مباشرة • تستلم المبلغ من الزبون مباشرة، بدون نسبة علينا رابط الدفع: stripe.com/pay/… بعد الدفع يتفعّل متجرك خلال دقائق.
    You receive
    💳 طلب تفعيل متجر أحمد العامري — ahmed واتساب: 971508400886 أُرسل له رابط الدفع (55 د.إ / شهر).
  5. ⏸

    Then nothing happens

    minutes · or days · or never

    The flow ends here. They are registered, their page works, their store does not. Nothing is waiting on a timer — the second half only starts if money moves.

  6. 5

    Stripe calls back

    Stripe says it was paid

    Stripe posts every event it has. Read the Stripe event keeps only checkout.session.completed and invoice.paid, and only when the status really is paid. Everything else gets a polite 200 and is dropped.

  7. 6

    The store switches on

    Switch the store on

    One row update: store_status=active, plan=store, and next_renewal exactly one month out. That date is what flow 3 lives on.

    Seller receives
    تم استلام الدفعة ✅ متجرك شغّال الحين: rentstore.ae/@ahmed التجديد القادم: 2026-09-21 أرسل لنا منتجاتك (صورة، اسم، سعر، وصف قصير) ونضيفها لك، أو أضفها بنفسك من لوحة التحكم.
    You receive
    💰 اشتراك جديد مدفوع ahmed — 55 AED التجديد القادم: 2026-09-21

Asking is not paying

A store only becomes active on a Stripe callback — never on the request. Anyone can ask; only a payment flips the row. That single rule is what keeps the subscription honest without you checking anything by hand.

3 · The month comes around again

03-subscription-renewal.json
Trigger Schedule — 09:00 Asia/Dubai, every day · no webhook, no button
  1. 1

    Read every seller, work out what today means

    Who needs what today?

    For each active store it compares next_renewal to today and sorts them into four buckets. A seller in none of them is simply skipped — most mornings, that is everyone.

    WhenBucketWhat fires
    −3 daysreminderFriendly heads-up, no link
    day 0due_todayPayment link
    +3 daysoverdueWarning, store still running
    +7 dayssuspendStore off, page stays
  2. 2

    Three days before

    WhatsApp: renewal in 3 days
    Seller receives
    تذكير ودّي 🗓️ اشتراك متجرك يتجدد بعد ٣ أيام (2026-09-21) — 55 د.إ. ما عليك سوّي شي إذا الدفع تلقائي. وإذا تبي توقف الاشتراك رد بكلمة (إيقاف) قبل التاريخ.
  3. 3

    On the day, and three days late

    WhatsApp ×2 branches

    The due-day message carries the payment link. The late one carries the link and the consequence — and says the part that matters most:

    Seller receives
    متجرك متأخر ٣ أيام عن التجديد ⏳ رابط الدفع: stripe.com/pay/… بعد ٧ أيام من تاريخ التجديد يتوقف المتجر مؤقتاً — بس صفحتك وروابطك تبقى شغالة مجاناً وما تروح.
  4. 4

    After the grace period, the store switches off

    Suspend the store

    The row goes to suspended. Their products stay in the sheet, their page and links keep working on the free plan. Nothing is deleted, ever — one payment brings the whole store back exactly as it was.

    Seller receives
    تم إيقاف المتجر مؤقتاً صفحتك وروابطك ما زالت شغالة: rentstore.ae/@ahmed منتجاتك محفوظة كما هي. أي وقت تدفع، يرجع المتجر بنفس المنتجات: stripe.com/pay/…
  5. 5

    You get one line per person, not a report

    WhatsApp: daily line to the owner
    You receive
    📋 متابعة الاشتراكات ahmed — متأخر ٣ أيام التجديد: 2026-09-21

    On a quiet morning, nothing arrives at all. That silence is the useful part — a message means something needs you.

Suspending, not deleting, is the business decision

A suspended seller is still on the platform, still sending people to a page with your name at the bottom, and one tap from paying again. A deleted seller is gone. The flow is built around the first.

4 · A customer buys something

04-customer-order.json
Trigger Checkout POSTs the cart to /webhook/rentstore-order
  1. 1

    The cart arrives as an order

    Build the order

    Line totals are recalculated here, never trusted from the browser. It mints an id like ORD-260821-4821 and normalises the customer's number the same way flow 1 does.

    Payload
    { "handle": "_ys", "seller_phone": "971508400886", "customer_name": "سالم", "customer_phone": "0551234567", "items": [ { "title": "عود ملكي", "qty": 1, "price": 150 } ] }
  2. 2

    Written down before anyone is told

    Write it to the orders sheet

    Status new. The sheet is the record — if a WhatsApp message fails, the order still exists.

  3. 3

    The seller gets everything needed to fulfil it

    WhatsApp: new order for the seller
    Seller receives
    🛒 طلب جديد ORD-260821-4821 1× عود ملكي — 150 د.إ الإجمالي: 150 د.إ الزبون: سالم رقمه: 971551234567 رد على الزبون واتفق معه على التوصيل والدفع.
  4. 4

    The customer gets a receipt — if they left a number

    Did the customer leave a number?

    Both cart fields are optional. Empty means this branch is skipped cleanly; the order still reaches the seller.

    Customer receives
    شكراً سالم 🌟 وصل طلبك ORD-260821-4821: 1× عود ملكي — 150 د.إ الإجمالي: 150 د.إ البائع بيتواصل معك قريب لتأكيد التوصيل والدفع.
  5. ⏱

    24 hours pass

    Wait 24 hours

    the workflow sleeps, then wakes on this exact order

    It re-reads the row. If you or the seller changed the status to done in the sheet, nothing happens. If it still says new:

    Seller receives
    تذكير: الطلب ORD-260821-4821 صار له ٢٤ ساعة بدون رد. الزبون: سالم — 971551234567 الإجمالي: 150 د.إ

You are not in this conversation

No message in this flow goes to you, and no money passes through the platform — the customer pays the seller directly. You charge the seller a monthly fee and nothing else. That is exactly what the site's FAQ promises, and it is why running this needs no payment licence.

5 · Full setup, licence included

05-setup-and-licence.json
Two triggers /webhook/rentstore-setup-request then, repeatedly, /webhook/rentstore-setup-stage
  1. 1

    Someone picks the full setup

    Setup requested

    The same signup form, but the full-setup plan routes to a different webhook — one form, two flows behind it. This is the only one that runs for days, so it opens a case with a stage rather than finishing in a second.

    Becomes
    { "case_id": "SETUP-260821-104", "stage": "new", "phone": "971509876543", "fee": 1500 }
  2. 2

    The customer learns exactly what to prepare

    WhatsApp: what happens next
    Customer receives
    أهلاً سارة 👋 استلمنا طلب التأسيس (SETUP-260821-104). الخطوة الأولى مكالمة ٢٠ دقيقة — مجانية — نحدد فيها الإمارة ونوع الرخصة، ونعطيك رقم الرسوم الحكومية المؤكد قبل ما تدفع أي شي. جهّز لنا إذا تقدر: • صورة جواز السفر (والإقامة والهوية إن وجدت) • صورة شخصية بخلفية بيضاء • ٣ أسماء تجارية مقترحة بالترتيب • وصف نشاطك: وش تبيع بالضبط رسومنا 1500 د.إ مرة واحدة وتشمل سنة اشتراك في المتجر. الرسوم الحكومية تدفعها للجهة مباشرة.
  3. 3

    You get a numbered job, not a notification

    WhatsApp: your to-do list
    You receive
    🏷️ طلب تأسيس جديد SETUP-260821-104 سارة — 971509876543 النشاط: عطور المطلوب منك: ١. رتّب مكالمة الـ٢٠ دقيقة ٢. حدد الإمارة ونوع الرخصة ٣. أكّد رقم الرسوم الحكومية قبل ما يدفع ٤. حدّث stage في الشيت وتنرسل له الحالة تلقائياً
  4. ☎

    The call, and the paperwork

    day 1 · then 3–10 working days at the authority

    This part is human, and it stays human: you agree the emirate and the licence type, quote the real government fee, collect the documents, and file through your setup partner. The automation's job is that the customer never has to ask where things stand.

  5. 4

    You move the case; the customer hears it

    Stage updated

    One POST per stage — docs_received, filed, licence_issued, store_live — and the matching message goes out in Arabic without you writing it.

    You POST
    { "case_id": "SETUP-260821-104", "phone": "971509876543", "stage": "filed" }
    Customer receives
    قدّمنا طلبك للجهة 📤 (SETUP-260821-104) غالباً ٣ إلى ١٠ أيام عمل، وتختلف حسب الإمارة والنشاط. أي رد أو ملاحظة من الجهة نبلغك فيها أول بأول.
  6. 5

    Licence issued, then the store opens

    WhatsApp ×2 stages
    Customer receives
    رخصتك صدرت 🎉 (SETUP-260821-104) نرسل لك نسخة الرخصة الحين، ونبدأ فوراً تجهيز صفحتك ومتجرك ورفع منتجاتك.
    Then, days later
    متجرك فتح 🛍️ (SETUP-260821-104) كل شي جاهز: صفحتك، متجرك، ومنتجاتك. باقي جلسة التدريب — قل لنا الوقت المناسب لك. اشتراكك في المتجر مدفوع سنة كاملة.

Two promises this flow is built to keep

No fee number before the call. Government fees differ by emirate, licence type, and activity, and they change. Every message says the confirmed number comes on the first call, before any payment — so never quote one from memory.

You do not issue licences. The authority does; you prepare, file, and follow up through a licensed setup partner. Every message says so, because promising an outcome you don't control is how setup agents collect complaints.

The customer ends with

  • A trade licence in their own name
  • A built store with their products in it
  • A year of subscription already paid
  • An hour of training

You end with

  • One fee, paid once, up front
  • A subscriber who cannot easily leave
  • Four POSTs of admin instead of a week of "any update?"

Two things that will bite you

WhatsApp's 24-hour window

Plain text only reaches someone within 24 hours of their last message to you. After that, approved templates only.

  • Flows 1, 2 and 4 are safe — the site's buttons make the person message you first, which opens the window
  • Flow 3 fires weeks later, so its reminders need Meta-approved templates
  • Messages to your own number are never affected

next_renewal must be a real date

Flow 3 skips any row it can't read a date from — silently, forever.

  • Flow 2 writes it automatically on payment
  • Activate someone by hand and you must type it yourself
  • Format: 2026-09-21

Still yours to do by hand

Building each seller's actual page. The automations record them, take their money, chase renewals, and route their orders — but the page itself you still make from the row in the sheet. Automating that is the moment this needs a real backend, and it is the next build, not this one.
